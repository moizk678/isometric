**[E] Icon construction:** one coherent outline family; 20/24px view boxes, rounded caps/joins, consistent stroke weight. Do not mix filled emoji, multicolor illustrations, and unrelated icon libraries. Optical centering may shift an asymmetric icon by about 1px. Icons conveying the same role must be reused rather than redrawn differently. Icon-only actions require meaningful accessible names; decorative status icons accompanying equivalent text should not duplicate the announcement.

## Asset provenance and limits

The insurer logos and profile image are embedded raster evidence. Exact recreation needs the actual logo/photo assets and font. Do not invent exact SVG paths or identify an icon library as fact. In arbitrary software, use the actual organizations' supplied marks, or neutral name/initial capsules. Keep wordmarks in original colors inside white pills, preserve aspect ratio with contain fitting, and give tiny artwork enough internal whitespace. Avoid enlarging micro-logos until their embedded text becomes blurry.
